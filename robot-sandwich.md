# Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The bread, peanut butter, jelly, knife, and plate are already laid out in front of you
- All of the ingredients are still in their packaging
- The bread is in a bag with a twist tie sealing it shut
- Both the peanut butter and jelly are in seperate jars

## How to make the sandwich
1. Move the plate to sit directly in front of you on the counter
2. Pick up the bag of bread
3. Untwist the twist tie and set it down next to the plate
4. While holding the bag of bread in one hand, reach through the opening with the other hand and pull out exactly two slices of bread
5. Set the 2 slices of bread on the plate
6. Pick up the twist tie and use it to reseal the bag of bread
7. Set the bag of bread back down in its original position
8. Pick up the jar of peanut butter
9. Unscrew the lid and set it down next to the plate
10. While holding the jar in one hand, use the other hand to grab the knife by the handle with the blade facing upwards
11. Use the blade end of the knife to reach into the jar and scoop out some peanut butter
12. Set the jar of peanut butter down
13. With your now free hand, grab one of the slices of bread from the plate
14. Using the blade end of the knife, spread the peanut butter onto one side of the slice of bread
15. Set the bread back down onto the plate with the peanut butter side facing up
16. Set the knife down in its original position
17. Pick up the jar of peanut butter in one hand and the lid in the other
18. Screw the lid back onto the jar of peanut butter to seal it
19. Set the jar of peanut butter back in its original position
20. Grab the jar of jelly
21. Unscrew the lid and set it next to the plate
22. Grab the knife by the handle with the blade facing up
23. Using the blade end of the knife, reach into the jar and scoop out some jelly
24. Set the jar of jelly down
25. With your now free hand, grab the slice of bread that does not have peanut butter on it
26. Using the blade end of the knife, spread some jelly onto one side of the slice of bread
27. Set the knife down in its original position
28. Flip the slice of bread with the jelly over so the jelly side is facing down
29. Place the slice of bread with the jelly on top of the slice of bread with peanut butter
30. Grab the jar of jelly and the lid
31. Screw the lid back onto the jar
32. Set the jar of jelly back in its original position

## Done when
- There are 2 slices of bread stacked on top of eachother with peanut butter and jelly in between them
- All ingredients are sealed and set back in their original positions
